# Images

The photos in `src/assets/images/` were generated with AI for this
fictional site. They do not show real places, people or products.

- **Model:** Google Gemini Pro (image generation), October 2026
- **Prompts:** one shared style description plus one prompt per image,
  kept in the maintainer's brief for this template
- **Processing:** cropped to their aspect ratio and scaled to at most
  1600 px wide; the site converts them to WebP at build time
- **Licence:** like the rest of this repository, the images are under the
  [MIT licence](LICENSE). You may use, change and redistribute them,
  including in your own site.

Images from Gemini carry Google's invisible SynthID watermark, which marks
them as AI-generated.

The favicon, the tern logo and the harbour map are SVG drawings made for
this site, under the same licence.

## Video

The home page's background video, `public/videos/hero.mp4`, is a slow zoom
over `hero-interior` made with ffmpeg: 4 seconds, 640×360, no sound, H.264.
Its poster, `hero-poster.jpg`, is the first frame. Same licence as the
photos.
