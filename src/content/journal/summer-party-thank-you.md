Seven years ago this month we opened the door at 14 Quay Row with eight
tables, a second-hand espresso machine and no idea whether anyone would
come. On Saturday, about two hundred of you came to say happy birthday.

## The numbers, roughly

- 160 mackerel on the grill, all from the *Morwenna* and the *Silver Tern*
- 300 cardamom buns, which Teo started baking at three in the morning
- 14 kegs of Alder Valley cider
- 1 set of string lights that failed at 9 pm and was fixed by a stranger
  with a penknife, whom we would like to thank and pay in buns

## Thank you

To the Harbour Brass, who played for three hours and only stopped for
mackerel. To the harbour master, for letting us take over the quay. To
the farm, the dairy and Northlight, who are there every week and were
there on Saturday too. And to everyone who has come in, once or every
morning, over the past seven years. We couldn't do this anywhere else.

See you next summer. Same quay.
