When we opened, the first thing we had to decide was the coffee. The
second thing we found out was that the best roaster in the area was in an
old net loft on Chapel Street, about four minutes' walk from our door.

## Northlight

Northlight is two people, Jess and Callum, and a twelve-kilo roaster that
they bought from a café that was closing down. They roast four days a
week, mostly to order, and they deliver to us on foot.

They buy directly where they can, and they keep a short list: a house
espresso blend, a decaf, and a single origin for filter that changes with
the seasons.

## What we pour

- **Espresso, cortado and flat white:** the house blend, currently a
  Brazilian and Colombian blend roasted for milk. Chocolate, hazelnut,
  not much acid.
- **Filter:** whatever single origin Jess is most excited about. At the
  moment it's a washed Ethiopian that tastes of peach tea.
- **Decaf:** a Colombian sugarcane decaf. You can have it in anything.

## Why it matters

Coffee loses most of what makes it good within a few weeks of roasting.
Ours is never more than ten days old, and usually about five. You can
taste it, especially in the filter.

And when the grinder breaks on a Saturday morning, Callum comes round
with a spare. Try getting that from a wholesaler.
