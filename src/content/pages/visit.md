## Getting here

We're on Quay Row, the street that runs along the harbour wall, three
doors down from the harbour master's office.

- **By bus:** the 41 from the station stops at Harbour Square, two
  minutes' walk.
- **By car:** there's a pay-and-display car park on the quay. It fills up
  early on summer weekends; the long-stay on Hill Road is a ten-minute
  walk downhill.
- **By bike:** racks outside the door.

## Accessibility

The entrance is step-free, and so is the terrace. We have an accessible
toilet on the ground floor. Tables are movable, so tell us if you need
more room and we'll make it. The menu is on this website if you'd rather
read it ahead.
