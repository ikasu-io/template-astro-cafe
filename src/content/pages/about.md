Maren grew up in Port Alder and left at eighteen to cook in the city. Ten
years and three restaurant kitchens later, she came home for a summer and
didn't go back. Teo came with her. He'd been baking since he was a child,
mostly his grandmother's recipes, and was ready to do it properly.

In the spring of 2019 the old chandlery at 14 Quay Row came up for rent.
It had a big window onto the harbour, a kitchen that needed everything
replacing and a flat upstairs. We took it, spent the summer rebuilding
it, and opened in August with eight tables and a second-hand espresso
machine.

## How we work

**We bake every morning.** Teo starts at six. Everything on the bakery
counter is made here, from the sourdough to the blondies.

**We cook what's in season.** The menu changes with the farm and the
boats, so some things are only on for a few weeks. When something runs
out, it's gone until tomorrow.

**We buy close to home.** Almost everything comes from within fifteen
miles, and most of it from the four suppliers below.

**We waste as little as we can.** Yesterday's bread becomes croutons,
crumbs and bread pudding. What we can't use goes to the Port Alder food
bank at closing time.
