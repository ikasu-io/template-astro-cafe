Once a month we push the tables together, light the candles and cook a
proper dinner. October's is a harvest supper: the last tomatoes, the first
squash, apples from the valley and whatever the boats bring in that
morning.

The menu is five courses, set by Maren on the Wednesday before. We will
write to you with it, and you can tell us then about anything you don't
eat. Expect bread, a lot of it.

- Arrival from 18:45, we sit down at 19:00
- Five courses, £48 per person; wine and cider by the glass or bottle
- 24 places at one long table, so you may well make friends

hello@terntide.example. We ask for payment when you book, and refund it
up to a week before.
