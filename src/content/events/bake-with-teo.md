Teo has made about forty thousand cardamom buns since we opened. On this
Saturday morning he will show eight of you how, before the café opens.

You'll make the dough, shape the knots (it takes a few goes, everyone's
first one looks like a pretzel), and bake them in our oven. Coffee and a
bun while yours prove. You go home with six buns, the recipe and a jar of
our cardamom sugar.

- 8:00 to 10:30, in the café kitchen
- £35 per person, all ingredients included
- Eight places; ages 12 and up with an adult
