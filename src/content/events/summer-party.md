Seven summers on Quay Row. We closed the kitchen at four, hung the lights
along the harbour wall and grilled mackerel until it got dark.

Thank you to everyone who came, to the Harbour Brass for playing, and to
the harbour master for letting us borrow the quay. There's a write-up
[in the journal](/journal/summer-party-thank-you).
