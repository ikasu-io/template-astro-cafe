On the shortest day we'll stay open late, light every lantern we own and
put a pot of mulled cider on. It's the one Monday in the year we open.

Bring a lantern if you have one; the children's lantern walk sets off
along the quay at half five. Cider, hot chocolate and buns are on us
until they run out.

No booking needed. Dogs welcome, as always on the terrace.
